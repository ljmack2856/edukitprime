Sub-strands

Locking keeps your chosen lessons for a sub-strand when the scheme is recalculated. Restore returns one sub-strand to the design’s suggestion without touching your other choices. Entering 0 leaves a sub-strand out, and it is labelled as such. The box marked pg. takes the textbook pages for that sub-strand. Methods sets the assessment methods for that sub-strand only.
